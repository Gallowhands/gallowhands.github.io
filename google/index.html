room

room is a personal program that runs on one person's own computer. It is not a service, it has no other users, and it is not offered to anyone.

It signs in to its owner's own Gmail account, with his permission, to help him sort his inbox: it notices which senders he reads and which he never opens, and suggests unsubscribing. It changes nothing in the mailbox unless he presses a key to do it.
