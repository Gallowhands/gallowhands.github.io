room: privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026 (reading mail and the calendar added).

room is a personal program used only by its owner, on his own computer, with his own Google account.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, through the Gmail API, it reads message headers (sender, recipients, subject, date, labels such as read or unread, and the List-Unsubscribe header) and Gmail's one-line preview of each message. When the owner opens a message to read it, room fetches that message's text to show it; that text is kept in memory only and never written to disk. It does not download attachments.

Through the Google Calendar API it reads the owner's calendar, read-only, to show what's coming up and to remind him before events.

Where it goes

Everything it keeps is stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted with Windows' per-user encryption (it opens only for his Windows account on that machine). It is not sent to any server, not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising. No one but the owner has access to it.

What it changes

Nothing, unless the owner presses a key to act: unsubscribing from a sender (using the sender's own List-Unsubscribe mechanism), starring, marking as read, labelling or archiving a message, or keeping a sender out of his inbox (which archives their mail). It never deletes mail and never changes the calendar.

Google user data

room's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can withdraw room's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete what it stored by deleting its folder on his computer.
