room: privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

room is a personal program used only by its owner, on his own computer, with his own Google account.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, through the Gmail API, it reads message headers: sender, subject, date, labels (such as read or unread), whether he replied, and the List-Unsubscribe header. It does not read message bodies or attachments.

Where it goes

Everything it reads is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server, not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising. No one but the owner has access to it.

What it changes

Nothing, unless the owner presses a key to act: unsubscribing from a sender (using the sender's own List-Unsubscribe mechanism), or labelling or archiving a message. It never deletes mail.

Google user data

room's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can withdraw room's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete what it stored by deleting its folder on his computer.
